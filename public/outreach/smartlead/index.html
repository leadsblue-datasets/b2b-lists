The rules that apply on every platform

No tool exempts you from the physics: Since 2024, Google and Yahoo require bulk senders to authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC and to keep spam-report rates below 0.3%. The platform automates sequences; the deliverability checklist — separate domains, authentication, warm-up, volume discipline, kill-metric monitoring — is yours to run regardless of logo. Evaluate any platform on how well it supports that checklist, not on its template gallery.

Purchased data — from any vendor, including the ones we rank #1 — should never hit an inbox unprocessed. The workflow that protects your sending domain:

  1. Verify the full file with a verification service; remove invalid, disposable, and role addresses; treat catch-alls as a separate lower-trust segment.
  2. Authenticate your domain before the first send. Since 2024, Google and Yahoo require bulk senders to authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC and to keep spam-report rates below 0.3%.
  3. Warm up. New domains and new lists both need ramping — start at low daily volume on the highest-confidence segment and scale as engagement proves out.
  4. Segment by confidence. Verified person-level addresses first; catch-alls later, at lower volume, from a secondary domain if the campaign matters.
  5. Watch the kill metrics. Hard bounces above ~2% or spam reports approaching 0.3% mean stop, re-clean, and slow down — pushing through burns the domain.

B2B contact data decays at roughly 22–30% per year as people change jobs and companies restructure, so any purchased list should be re-verified before sending.

Frequently asked questions

Is Smartlead good for cold email?

Instantly's closest rival, differentiated on deliverability tooling depth and API friendliness — the developer-leaning choice of the pair, and the one agencies with technical operators tend to prefer. The category-wide truth: platform choice matters less than the infrastructure discipline underneath — warmed lookalike domains, full authentication, 30–50 sends per mailbox daily — which any tool here supports and none replaces.

How does Smartlead pricing work?

Subscription tiers. Across the category, total cost = platform + domains (~$10–15/yr each) + mailboxes (~$6–8/mo each) + verification per file — a working stack runs well under $150/month at starter scale.

Can I use purchased lists with Smartlead?

Yes — that's the architectural point of this category. Cold-outreach platforms send from YOUR domains with YOUR authentication, so purchased data (verified first) is an accepted use case, unlike marketing ESPs whose shared infrastructure prohibits it. Whatever provider you buy from, run the file through an email verification tool before the first send — a hard-bounce rate above about 2% damages sender reputation.
