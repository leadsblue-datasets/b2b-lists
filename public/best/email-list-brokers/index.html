How to choose for buyers specifically seeking brokers

If you genuinely need a broker — custom cuts, managed files, multi-channel campaigns — run the process like procurement: identical spec to two or three brokers, free samples from each, verify the samples, compare per-record cost on survivors, and get the replacement policy in writing. If you're broker-shopping only because you assumed quotes were how lists are bought, the published-price catalogs at the top of this ranking solve the same job in minutes. Our full 33-broker review index covers every major broker individually.

B2B contact data decays at roughly 22–30% per year as people change jobs and companies restructure, so any purchased list should be re-verified before sending. Whatever provider you buy from, run the file through an email verification tool before the first send — a hard-bounce rate above about 2% damages sender reputation.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Buying on database size. "250 million records" is a marketing number; what matters is verified coverage of your segment. A 5,000-record file that's 97% valid beats 50,000 stale rows every time.
  • Skipping verification because the seller "guarantees accuracy." Guarantees compensate you after the bounce damage is done; verification prevents it.
  • Sending the whole file on day one. Volume spikes from a new domain are the fastest route to the spam folder regardless of data quality.
  • Ignoring destination-country law. A file that's legal to buy isn't automatically legal to mail everywhere; B2C rules especially vary.
  • Comparing a subscription price to a file price. They're different products. Compare cost-per-usable-contact-per-campaign instead.

Frequently asked questions

What does a list broker do?

A broker sells compiled contact files by segment, usually via custom quotes. Most traditional list brokers use quote-based pricing: you request counts for your segment and receive a custom price, rather than seeing published per-record rates. See our full broker review index.

How do I compare list brokers?

Identical spec to multiple brokers, samples from each, verify the samples, compare per-record price on the files that survive. Sample verification outranks every marketing claim.
