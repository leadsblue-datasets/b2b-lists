Frequently Asked Questions

Is my campaign data kept confidential?

Yes. All submissions are anonymised before inclusion. We store only the performance metrics — open rate, reply rate, bounce rate — not any personal data or contact lists. Your email is used only for verification and is never published.

How is my submission used?

Your submission is aggregated into the B2B Data Index benchmark dataset. After validation, it improves the benchmark ranges for your country and industry combination. The dataset is published at /api/v1/benchmark-data.json under CC BY 4.0 — your contribution is part of the global reference used by outbound sales teams worldwide.

What do I get for contributing?

Immediately: your results benchmarked against the aggregate for your country and industry — you see exactly how you compare to the market. Your submission also improves the benchmark that thousands of other practitioners reference when planning campaigns.
