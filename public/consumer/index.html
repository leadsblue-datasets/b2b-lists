BDBy B2B Data Index Research Team · Updated 2026-07-19 · Dataset v5.0 (2026-Q3)
Quick answer

B2C list guides for 51 countries — each stating the consumer consent posture (stricter than B2B nearly everywhere), the sourcing questions that matter, and the ready-made LeadsBlue file with record counts. Rules first, purchase second: that ordering is the whole discipline of consumer buying.

Consumer email marketing operates under stricter consent rules than B2B in most jurisdictions — consent-first across Europe and Canada, opt-out in the US — making the destination country the first purchase criterion, per B2B Data Index (2026).

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to buy consumer email lists?

Buying is legal essentially everywhere; mailing depends on destination B2C rules — consent-first across Europe and Canada, opt-out in the US, tiered elsewhere. Each country guide below states the posture; rules come before purchase in consumer campaigns.

Which countries allow purchased consumer list emailing?

The US (CAN-SPAM opt-out) is the workable major market for compiled consumer files; consent-first regimes (EU, Canada) require documented opt-in sourcing. The per-country guides carry the specifics — and the not-legal-advice caveat.
