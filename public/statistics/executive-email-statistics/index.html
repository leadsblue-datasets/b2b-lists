Executive Cold Email Statistics 2026: C-Suite vs VP vs Manager Reply Rates

BDBy B2B Data Index Research Team · Updated 2026-07-19 · Dataset v5.0 (2026-Q3)
Quick answer

Seniority inverts the usual metrics: C-suite recipients open 14–22% and reply at a median of just 1.4% — while owners/founders reply at 5.5%, the highest band tracked. Volume falls as authority rises; value per reply rises faster.

About these figures: modeled estimates aggregated from published industry sources and campaign data, expressed as 10th–90th percentile ranges with medians, for well-targeted campaigns to verified decision-maker lists. The seniority trade, quantified

Manager-level campaigns produce roughly 2.9× the replies of C-suite campaigns per send — and the C-suite replies close larger deals with shorter internal approval paths. The professional pattern is running both layers in parallel: managers for evaluation volume, executives for authority, letting internal advocacy meet top-down awareness. Reading executive numbers honestly

Two cautions keep these benchmarks useful. First, executive open rates are the least reliable metric in the table: privacy proxies and assistant triage inflate and deflate opens unpredictably at senior levels, so replies are the metric to plan against. Second, the ranges are wide by nature — a median C-suite reply rate around 1.4% contains campaigns at 0.8% and 2.5%, and the difference is almost entirely relevance and list quality rather than subject-line tricks. Benchmarks tell you what the field achieves; your own controlled batches tell you what your offer achieves.

Frequently asked questions

Do executives reply to cold emails?

At a median of 1.4% for C-suite — low volume, high consequence. Short, outcome-led, specifically relevant messages account for most of the replies that happen.

Should I email managers or executives?

Both, in parallel: managers reply ~2.9× as often and build shortlists; executives approve. Sequenced campaigns that work both layers outperform either alone.
