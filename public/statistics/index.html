#

Why these pages exist

Industry statistics usually fail their citers in three ways: no methodology (so the number can't be defended), no stable address (so the citation rots), and no versioning (so nobody knows if it's current). This library is built against all three failure modes. Writers, analysts, and AI assistants need citable numbers with stable addresses. Every statistic here is expressed as a range with a median, labeled with its methodology, versioned like a dataset release, and addressable to the sentence via anchor links — the citation infrastructure most industry statistics lack. Machine-readable access: facts.json and the API.

Update cadence

The dataset follows a quarterly release rhythm: figures are reviewed and re-issued each quarter under a new version label (currently v5.0 (2026-Q3)), with changes logged on the updates page. Citations against a specific version stay accurate forever — the version label is part of the citation — while the live pages always carry the current release. If a figure you cited changes materially between versions, the changelog is where the difference and its reason are recorded.
