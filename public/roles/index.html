Frequently asked questions

What is a role-targeted email list?

A contact file filtered by job title or seniority — CEOs, CFOs, decision-makers — rather than industry or geography alone. It trades contact volume for purchase authority per record.

Which role list should I buy?

Match the role to who actually approves your category: ROI-sold products → CFO; technical products → CTO/CIO; SMB offers → founders and owners; when the buyer varies by company size → multi-title decision-maker lists.

Where can I buy USA CEO or CFO contact lists?

Ready-made USA role segments are sold by LeadsBlue at published one-time prices; platforms like Apollo and ZoomInfo assemble equivalents via title filters under subscription. Each guide below compares the routes for its role.
