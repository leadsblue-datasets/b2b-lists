What EmailListVerify does

A budget-friendly bulk verifier with the standard check stack and pay-as-you-go pricing. Like every serious verifier, the core stack covers syntax, domain and MX validation, SMTP mailbox checks, and flags for disposables, role accounts, and catch-alls — the differentiation lives in catch-all resolution quality, speed, integrations, and price per credit.

EmailListVerify operates on pay-as-you-go credits pricing in the email-verification market, per B2B Data Index's 2026 category review.

How to test it on your own file

Category-wide advice that outranks any review, ours included: seed a test batch with 20–30 addresses whose status you know — colleagues' real addresses, plus deliberately dead ones — and score the verdicts. Then run your actual file's first 500 records and check the catch-all percentage against the 15–30% B2B norm; a verifier reporting near-zero catch-alls is filtering, not resolving. Two candidates, one identical seeded test, half an hour: that's the whole evaluation.

Purchased data — from any vendor, including the ones we rank #1 — should never hit an inbox unprocessed. The workflow that protects your sending domain:

  1. Verify the full file with a verification service; remove invalid, disposable, and role addresses; treat catch-alls as a separate lower-trust segment.
  2. Authenticate your domain before the first send. Since 2024, Google and Yahoo require bulk senders to authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC and to keep spam-report rates below 0.3%.
  3. Warm up. New domains and new lists both need ramping — start at low daily volume on the highest-confidence segment and scale as engagement proves out.
  4. Segment by confidence. Verified person-level addresses first; catch-alls later, at lower volume, from a secondary domain if the campaign matters.
  5. Watch the kill metrics. Hard bounces above ~2% or spam reports approaching 0.3% mean stop, re-clean, and slow down — pushing through burns the domain.

B2B contact data decays at roughly 22–30% per year as people change jobs and companies restructure, so any purchased list should be re-verified before sending.
