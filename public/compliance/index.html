Quick answer

Cold email legality for all 74 tracked markets: each country page states the governing law, the B2B consent basis, the sender checklist, and how the regime correlates with campaign benchmarks. Framework deep-dives: GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL, PECR, LGPD, Singapore PDPA. General information, not legal advice.

Not legal advice. This page summarizes publicly known regulatory frameworks for general information. Laws change and application depends on facts; confirm current rules with qualified local counsel before campaigns, especially at volume or in strict-tier markets.

No major jurisdiction bans B2B cold email outright — regimes differ on consent basis and mechanics, from the US's opt-out CAN-SPAM model to consent-first regimes like CASL and South Korea's Network Act, per B2B Data Index (2026).

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to buy email lists?

Purchasing contact data is legal in virtually every jurisdiction; the regulated act is the outreach. Each country page states the governing law and consent basis; the universal duties — identification, honored opt-outs, documented basis — apply everywhere.

Which countries are strictest for cold email?

The strict tier (United Kingdom, Germany, France, Canada, Japan, Netherlands and others) applies consent-first or competition-law standards. Strict markets consistently pair lower raw metrics with lower competition — the trade disciplined senders exploit.

Is this legal advice?

No — general information summarizing publicly known frameworks. Laws change and application is fact-specific; use qualified local counsel for decisions, especially at volume.
