#

Why annotations, not just templates

Template libraries fail their users in a predictable way: the words get copied and the reasoning gets left behind, producing thousands of identical "quick question" emails that trained every executive's delete reflex. The annotations here exist to transfer the reasoning — why the observation opens, why one number beats three claims, why the ask descends across a sequence — so the templates can be rewritten in your voice for your segment and still work. Copied verbatim with placeholders half-filled, they will perform exactly like every other template on the internet.

The rule underneath every template

Under 120 words, relevance in line one, one claim with one number, an ask lighter than a meeting — and infrastructure underneath: Whatever provider you buy from, run the file through an email verification tool before the first send — a hard-bounce rate above about 2% damages sender reputation. The writing guide covers the craft; the deliverability reference covers the physics.
